Turn Surplus Stock into Smart Opportunities

StockRescue AI is an AI-powered inventory recovery agent designed to help small retailers reduce unsold stock, minimize product waste, and improve profitability. It identifies excess and slow-moving inventory, estimates future demand, compares recovery strategies, and connects surplus stock with potential demand from nearby shops.

By combining data analysis with AI-driven recommendations, StockRescue AI supports smarter inventory decisions and encourages sustainable retail practices.

🚨 Problem Statement

Small retailers often lose money because products remain unsold, expire, or occupy valuable storage space. At the same time, nearby shops may experience shortages of the same products.

Many small businesses lack affordable tools to forecast demand, assess stock risks, compare recovery options, and coordinate inventory transfers. This leads to avoidable waste, lost revenue, and inefficient inventory management.

💡 Our Solution

StockRescue AI acts as an intelligent decision-support agent that analyzes inventory data and recommends suitable recovery actions.

The system helps retailers identify stock at risk, evaluate possible actions, and discover opportunities to redistribute surplus products to nearby shops.

✨ Key Features

- **Stock Risk Detection:** Identifies excess, slow-moving, and near-expiry products.
- **Demand Forecasting:** Estimates future demand using historical sales data.
- **Smart Recovery Recommendations:** Compares discounts, bundle offers, holding stock, and stock transfers.
- **Profit-Based Analysis:** Evaluates estimated recovery value, potential revenue, and transfer costs.
- **Nearby Shop Matching:** Identifies potential stock transfer opportunities based on product demand and availability.
- **AI-Powered Decision Support:** Uses an LLM-based agent to interpret analysis and explain recommendations.
- **Outcome Tracking:** Records actual results to support better future decisions.

⚙️ How It Works

1. **Input Inventory Data:** Retailers provide product details, stock quantities, prices, expiry dates where applicable, and sales history.
2. **Analyze Stock Risk:** The system identifies products that may remain unsold or expire before sale.
3. **Estimate Demand:** Historical sales data is used to estimate future product demand.
4. **Compare Recovery Options:** The agent evaluates possible strategies, including discounts, bundles, and transfers.
5. **Recommend Actions:** The system suggests an appropriate recovery strategy based on expected value and associated costs.
6. **Track Results:** Retailers record the outcomes to evaluate the effectiveness of recommendations.
